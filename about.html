Personal Biography Class of 2027

About Zhuoli Yang

I am currently a high school student (Class of 2027) living in Kunming, Yunnan, China. Growing up surrounded by diverse natural landscapes, I developed a passion for hands-on biological exploration, comparative anatomy, and ecological observation early on.

Rather than just reading textbooks, I enjoy learning through direct practice—spending weeks cleaning and assembling a 1:1 Goliath Tigerfish skeleton bone by bone, maintaining planted vivarium ecosystems, caring for unique pet reptiles and scorpions, and performing classical saxophone distinction concertos.

Hands-on Skeletal Articulation
