Dianchi Wetland Ecosystem & Avian Wintering Dynamics

Location: Kunming Dianchi Riparian Wetlands

Multi-season field monitoring of wintering Black-Headed Gulls (Chroicocephalus ridibundus) migrating from Siberia. Correlated flock foraging density with aquatic vegetation belts, macrophyte coverage, and shoreline eutrophication mitigation efforts.

Avian Flight & Ethology Documented flock coordination, thermal soaring, and foraging dynamics.
Water Quality Indicators Observed correlation between benthic algae, macro-invertebrates, and fish health.
Subject: Chroicocephalus ridibundus Seasonal Ethology Log

Tropical Fieldwork · Southern Yunnan Canopy Ecology

Xishuangbanna Tropical Rainforest & Nocturnal Ethology

Location: Xishuangbanna Tropical Botanical Basin

Investigated primary tropical rainforest structural architecture: buttress root buttresses, strangler fig lattice enclosures, and cauliflorous flowering. Conducted nocturnal field walks utilizing 365nm UV torches to locate bio-fluorescent scorpions, phasmids, and praying mantises.

UV Bio-Fluorescence Excitation of beta-carboline molecules in scorpion cuticles under long-wave UV.
Canopy Epiphytes Micro-ecosystem humus baskets formed by *Asplenium nidus* (Bird's nest fern).
Habitat: Lowland Monsoon Rainforest Field Survey Log
