Field Hike · Western Yunnan Nature Trail

Gaoligongshan & Tiger Leaping Gorge Hike

Location: Tiger Leaping Gorge · Gaoligong Mountains

An unforgettable hiking journey through the dramatic valleys of Western Yunnan. We hiked along high trails overlooking the roaring rapids of Tiger Leaping Gorge on the Jinsha River, and walked beneath the cool forest canopy of Gaoligongshan enjoying the fresh air and mountain flora.

Gorge Cliff Trails Hiked along the narrow cliff pathways above roaring river rapids.
Mountain Flora Spotted wild orchids, giant fern fronds, and mossy trees on alpine paths.

Travel & Nature · Kunming Winter Visit

Wintering Gulls at Dianchi Lake

Location: Dianchi Lake Shoreline · Kunming, Yunnan

Every winter, thousands of black-headed gulls fly all the way from Siberia to spend the winter at Dianchi Lake in Kunming. Visiting the lake, I loved standing along the bank holding pieces of bread up in the air. The gulls are incredibly bold and skilled flyers, swooping down gently to grab the bread right out of my hand.

Hand-Feeding Experience Standing on the shore and feeling the rush of wings as gulls take bread from my fingers.
Winter Migration Learned how these migratory birds travel thousands of kilometers each year to Kunming.
Location: Dianchi Lake, Kunming Travel Memory

Travel & Nature · Southern Yunnan Rainforest Walk

Exploring the Tropical Rainforest of Xishuangbanna

Location: Xishuangbanna Botanical Basin · Southern Yunnan

A wonderful nature trip into the tropical rainforests of Xishuangbanna. We walked past towering buttress roots and winding strangler figs during the day, and set out on an exciting night walk with a UV flashlight to spot scorpions and insects glowing brightly in neon green against the dark tree bark.

Night Walk with UV Light Shining a UV flashlight onto hollow trunks and rocks to find scorpions glowing in the dark.
Rainforest Canopy Exploring massive buttress roots, aerial vines, and hanging ferns in the humid canopy.
Habitat: Lowland Tropical Rainforest Travel Journal
