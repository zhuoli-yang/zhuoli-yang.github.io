Hands-on DIY · Fish Anatomy Project #01

Building a Goliath Tigerfish Skeleton

Assembling a complete skeleton of an African Goliath Tigerfish by hand. To avoid damaging the fragile bones, I practiced cleaning and piecing together a common carp first. Once confident, I carefully cleaned, numbered, and glued every bone and sharp tooth into a display case.

Practicing First: Tested bone cleaning on a common market carp first so I wouldn't ruin the fragile tigerfish bones.
Painstaking Assembly: Aligned dozens of tiny vertebrae and set each sharp tooth back into its socket like a 3D jigsaw puzzle.

Plant & Tank Keeping · 3+ Years Setup #02

My Rainforest Vivarium & Planted Aquarium

Designing and maintaining a mini tropical ecosystem in my room. I planted live ferns and mosses, set up an automatic misting nozzle, and learned how to balance humidity, lighting, and water cleanliness so the plants and aquatic life stay healthy.

Balanced Microclimate: Programmed misting cycles and LED lighting to keep tropical mosses, ferns, and moisture-loving plants green and lush.
Tank Care & Patience: Tested water parameters regularly, cleaned filters, and learned to observe fish behavior to keep the aquarium healthy.

Pet Care · Desert Terrarium Pet #03

Keeping a Desert Scorpion

Setting up and caring for an arid desert scorpion at home. I created a natural habitat with fine sand, gravel, and bark shelters, fed it with long tweezers, and learned that scorpions are actually surprisingly calm and fascinating to watch.

Desert Habitat: Designed a low-humidity terrarium with sand, stone hides, and bark shelters suited for a desert animal.
Careful Feeding & Handling: Handled feeding with tweezers and observed how it uses its claws and tail safely without stressing it.

Pet Care · Reptiles Pet #04

Caring for My Pet Albino Corn Snake

Raising a friendly albino corn snake at home. It has a beautiful orange color and a very gentle temperament. I enjoyed holding it, watching how smoothly it climbs without legs, and keeping track of its regular meals and clean shedding cycles.

Docile & Friendly: Corn snakes make wonderful, quiet pets that love exploring, gently coiling around fingers for warmth.
Smooth Climbing: Watched how it grips branches effortlessly using its strong belly scales to climb without slipping.

Pet Care · Reptiles Pet #05

Raising a Pet Snapping Turtle

Raising a common snapping turtle from a small juvenile. I loved watching its rugged, spiky shell grow and observing how it stalks food and navigates underwater. Because snapping turtles have strong beaks, I learned how to handle it safely from behind the shell.

Distinctive Shell: Tracked its rapid growth from a palm-sized turtle with spiky ridges into an active, sturdy swimmer.
Safe Handling: Learned to always hold it securely near the back of its shell to keep both the turtle and my fingers safe.
