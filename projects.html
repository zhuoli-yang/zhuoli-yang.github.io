Osteology · Complete Specimen Specimen #01

Goliath Tigerfish Skeletal Reconstruction

Complete manual articulation of an apex predatory characin from the Congo basin. Involved precision cranial dissection, interlocking dentition articulation, and topological vertebra alignment according to degrees of joint freedom.

Dentition Mechanics: Reconstructed blade-like shearing teeth nestled into opposing jaw grooves with continuous replacement crypts.
Vertebral Kinematics: Numbered and strung along flexible steel armature preserving native lateral undulation curvature.

Empirical Calibration · Chemistry Specimen #02

NaOH Lipid Saponification Calibration

Systematic calibration performed on crucian carp (Carassius carassius) to determine exact concentration, duration, and temperature parameters for soft-tissue maceration before touching the valuable tigerfish specimen.

Serial Dilutions: Evaluated 0.5%, 1.0%, 2.0%, and 3.5% NaOH baths; documented that >2.0% erodes fine neural spines and softens opercular margins.
Optimal Threshold: Established 1.0% NaOH at ambient 22°C with 4-hour cycle inspections as the gold standard for preserving delicate sutures.

Applied Ethology · 3+ Years Habitat #01

Closed Vivarium Ecology & Differential Diagnostics

Long-term design and maintenance of controlled micro-habitats housing reptiles, arachnids, scorpions, and aquatic fauna. Formulated strict quarantine and differential symptom checklists.

Parasitic vs Bacterial Path: Differentiated between temperature-induced trophont emergence (*Ichthyophthirius*) requiring thermal ramps vs ammonia/nitrite intoxication treated by biological filter renewal.
Microclimate Balancing: Managed dual hygrometer gradients, bioactive springtail/isopod cleanup crews, and circadian lighting cycles.

Biophysics · Molecular Curiosity Study #01

Orthopteran Saltatorial Biomechanics

Investigating the biomechanical paradox of saltatorial insects: the instantaneous power output during takeoff exceeds the maximum contractile speed of striated muscle fibers by an order of magnitude.

Spring Mechanism: Co-contraction of slow extensor and fast flexor tibiae muscles bends cuticular semi-lunar processes, storing elastic energy prior to catch release.
Resilin Elasticity: High resilience (>97% energy return) polymer cushions high-frequency impacts, inspiring curiosity for future protein bioengineering.

Microscopy · Histochemistry Slide Series #01

Plant Histology & Vascular Cross-Sections

Freehand microtome sectioning of monocot and dicot stem specimens, followed by dual staining using Safranin-O (staining lignified xylem) and Fast Green FCF (staining cellulosic phloem).

Vascular Differentiation: Clear optical resolution between spiral and annular xylem tracheid thickenings and adjacent companion cells.
Slide Preparation: Practiced alcohol dehydration series and Canada balsam permanent mounting under glass coverslips.

Functional Morphology · Cuticle Specimen #06

Arthropod Cuticular Architecture & Microstructures

Optical and macro-photographic exploration of arachnid and insect cuticles: examining the protective epicuticular wax layer, sensory trichobothria arrays, and micro-grooved moisture channeling structures.

Arachnid Chelicerae: Serration geometry and venom duct openings observed on molted exuviae without harming live animals.
Structural Coloration: Examining how multi-layer interference produces metallic iridescence on coleopteran elytra.
