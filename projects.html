Applied Ethology · 3+ Years Habitat #02

Closed Vivarium Ecology & Differential Diagnostics

Long-term design and maintenance of controlled micro-habitats housing reptiles, arachnids, scorpions, and aquatic fauna. Formulated strict quarantine and differential symptom checklists.

Parasitic vs Bacterial Path: Differentiated between temperature-induced trophont emergence (*Ichthyophthirius*) requiring thermal ramps vs ammonia/nitrite intoxication treated by biological filter renewal.
Microclimate Balancing: Managed dual hygrometer gradients, bioactive springtail/isopod cleanup crews, and circadian lighting cycles.

Biophysics · Molecular Curiosity Inquiry #03

Orthopteran & Arthropod Biomechanics

Investigating the biomechanical paradox of arthropod joints: high-tensile chitinous sclerites, elastic resilin energy storage, and metasomal articulation under sudden directional strikes.

Spring Mechanism: Co-contraction of slow extensor and fast flexor tibiae muscles bends cuticular semi-lunar processes, storing elastic energy prior to catch release.
Resilin Elasticity: High resilience (>97% energy return) polymer cushions high-frequency impacts, inspiring curiosity for future protein bioengineering.

Herpetology · Serpentes Specimen #04

Albino Corn Snake Locomotion & Scale Kinematics

Biomechanical observation of rectilinear and lateral undulatory kinematics in Pantherophis guttatus. Explored how specialized overlapping beta-keratin scutes generate directional frictional anisotropy against substrates.

Frictional Anisotropy: Microscopic caudal-facing edges produce low forward sliding resistance but high reverse braking friction.
Axial Flexibility: Sequential muscle firing along hundreds of pre-cloacal vertebrae coordinating arboreal climbing purchase.

Herpetology · Testudines Specimen #05

Snapping Turtle Morphology & Ambush Biomechanics

Morphological inquiry into Chelydra serpentina: analyzing the evolutionary reduction of the plastron permitting expanded limb excursion alongside explosive cervical strike dynamics.

Plastron Reduction: Cross-shaped ventral shell minimizes mass, enabling agile aquatic walking along murky benthic riverbeds.
Cervical Strike Velocity: Highly flexible S-curved cervical vertebrae capable of rapid extension and jaw closure within milliseconds.
